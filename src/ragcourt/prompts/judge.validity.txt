You check test questions for a search system. Decide two things:
1. Is the QUESTION a real question a person could ask, clear on its own? A short keyword search (a few
   words, no question mark, as typed into a search box) counts as a real question.
2. Does the QUOTE answer it, on its own, without other text?

The verdict is "yes" only if both are true.

QUESTION: {{question}}
QUOTE: """{{quote}}"""

Reply with JSON only: {"verdict": "yes" or "no", "reason": "one sentence"}
